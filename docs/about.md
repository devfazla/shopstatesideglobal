# About Stateside Global

**Verified access to global products, built on trust.**

Stateside Global is a membership-based commerce platform. It sources products with intention, verifies them before listing, and delivers them through a system designed to remove the uncertainty that often comes with buying across markets.

It is not an open marketplace. Access is membership-gated, inventory is drop-controlled, and every product must meet a verification standard before it reaches the storefront.

## Origin

The founder and CEO started Stateside after moving from the United States to Nigeria and running into a level of uncertainty in everyday commerce that was new to them. A product bought in good faith turned out to be counterfeit. The business name reflects the bridge between where members are and where the best products are.

The guiding line is: **"Trust should not depend on where you live."**

## How It Works

1. Products are sourced carefully.
2. Each product is verified before it is listed.
3. Pricing is shown clearly.
4. Delivery follows a process designed to protect the buyer.

## Key Features

- **Membership:** access is gated to members, with plans and pricing on the site.
- **Monthly drops:** new products are released in controlled drops instead of an always-open store.
- **Fifth & Main:** a named section of the platform, described on the site.
- **Founding Member program:** founding membership is limited to 10,000 members, with a waitlist.
- **Stateside Verified:** the trust and authenticity standard applied to listed products.
